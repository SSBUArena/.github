### Super Smash Bros. Ultimate on PC, built for competitive online play.

Rollback netcode through a relay that never shows your address to anyone, a competitive 1v1 mode,
and one small launcher that sets everything up and keeps it up to date.

**[Download for Windows](https://github.com/ssbuarena/ssbu-arena/releases/latest/download/SSBU-Arena.exe)** ·
[Website](https://www.ssbuarena.com/) ·
[The project](https://www.ssbuarena.com/project) ·
[Source code](https://github.com/ssbuarena/ssbu-arena)

</div>

| | |
|---|---|
| 🌐 **Rollback online** | The game's own rooms over the internet, with rollback netcode |
| 🛡️ **Relay only** | Every packet goes through our server: no player ever sees another's IP |
| 🏆 **Competitive 1v1** | Legal stages only, no items, no Final Smash meter, no hazards |
| 🖥️ **One launcher** | Guided setup from your own copy of the game, automatic updates |

**In development:** friends, invites and Arena lobbies · automatic ranked 1v1 with skill-based ratings.

<sub>Independent community project, based on <a href="https://github.com/OpenSmash-Ultimate/ssbu-recomp">OpenSmash-Ultimate</a>. Not affiliated with Nintendo. No game code, assets, keys or firmware: you build it from your own copy.</sub>
